# Chapter One Environment Bible

This document defines the production environment language for Chapter One. Floors, edges and decals are original procedural raster art generated by project tools. Echo Yard props and landmarks are original AI-generated sheets that were chroma-keyed, sliced, visually reviewed and imported as transparent runtime sprites. AI style boards and unprocessed sheets remain source-only and are excluded from release exports.

## Shared Rules

- Camera: orthographic top-down; props may use shallow painted depth but cannot hide walkable cells.
- Grid: 64 x 64 pixels. Floor seams align to cell edges. Edge overlays use alpha and never alter collision.
- Readability: walkable surfaces stay between 18% and 42% luminance; cyan marks navigation or data objectives; amber marks physical hazards and industrial machinery.
- Material grouping: each map uses contiguous zones joined by thresholds, ramps, wall feet or drainage edges. Terrain IDs cannot be scattered as isolated decorative noise.
- Determinism: floor, edge, prop and decal variants are selected from the locked-map seed and cell coordinates.
- Cover: full cover has a dark continuous silhouette; half cover has a lower amber rim. Decorative landmarks do not create collision unless the locked map also contains a blocker cell.
- Reuse: neutral bolts, rail shadows, drain grates, cyan data lights and amber hazard paint may be reused. Primary landmarks, dominant material and route silhouette may not be reused between kits.

## Echo Freight Yard Vertical Slice

Reference: `assets/generated/chapter1/source/echo_yard_styleboard_v1.png`.

### Composition

- South: two-unit deployment apron with open sight lines and immediate half cover.
- Center: a two-cell-wide loading spine carrying cyan directional lights toward the terminal.
- West: container alleys provide the safest close-range flank but constrain long-range fire.
- East: a raised maintenance deck gives sight-line advantage and exposes units during the ramp approach.
- North: a crane control terminal gates the extraction pad under the gantry landmark.

### Runtime Component Contract

- 8 floor variants: clean plate, bolted plate, cyan guide, amber guide, grate, drain, worn plate, wet plate.
- 8 edge variants: north, east, south, west and four inner/outer corner overlays.
- 6 cover/prop variants: blue cargo, orange cargo, steel barrier, pipe bundle, cable spool, control crate.
- 3 decals: hazard chevrons, oil stain, wheel abrasion.
- 2 landmarks: gantry crane and floodlight tower.

### Tactical Contract

- Locked size: 14 x 10.
- Route anchors: west `(2,5)`, center `(7,5)`, east `(11,5)`.
- All three anchors must connect the deployment zone to extraction.
- Player and enemy spawn coordinates must be unique.
- Blocker ratio target: 10% to 24%; high-ground ratio target: 8% to 18%.
- First-turn enemies remain passive through the existing tutorial rule.

## Mag-Rail Transit Hub Production Kit

### Composition

- South: deployment concourse with two ticket-gate cover clusters and a clear first decision.
- West and east: parallel platform lanes that reward overwatch but expose units to cross-platform fire.
- Center: a magnetic-guide crossing gives the shortest extraction route and is deliberately dangerous.
- North: the suspended train nose marks the evacuation point; a signal gantry carries the high-ground threat.

### Runtime Component Contract

- 8 floor variants: ceramic slab, rail sleeper, signal-green guide strip, amber crossing, tactile platform edge, service grate, brake-dust slab and emergency lighting slab.
- 8 edge variants: north, east, south, west and four platform/crossing corner overlays.
- 6 cover/prop variants: ticket gate, platform bench, signal cabinet, rail bumper, luggage cart and maintenance bollard.
- 3 decals: platform arrows, electrical scorch and brake-dust streaks.
- 2 landmarks: suspended train nose and signal gantry.

### Tactical Contract

- `ch1_m3` locked size: 16 x 12; extract from the north platform.
- Route anchors: west `(3,6)`, crossing `(8,6)`, east `(12,6)`.
- At least 14% high ground across the signal gantry platform; the crossing remains visible from both elevated lanes.
- `ch1_m4` locked size: 16 x 14; escort the VIP through the east platform while the center crossing remains contestable.
- The two Transit Hub maps share components, not route layouts, blocker layouts or landmark placements.

## Sentinel Core Production Kit

### Composition

- Outer ring: dark alloy approach lanes with sparse cover that make position and cooldown timing legible.
- Middle ring: rotating-looking circuit bands and phase pylons create a visible breach decision without moving collision.
- Inner ring: a luminous cyan-white aperture marks the defended data objective or boss arena.
- Corruption: restrained crimson glitches increase toward the center; they are visual pressure, not unreadable noise.

### Runtime Component Contract

- 8 floor variants: black alloy, silver circuit, cyan circuit, crimson corruption, radial seam, phase plate, data grate and aperture glow.
- 8 edge variants: north, east, south, west and four radial corner overlays.
- 6 cover/prop variants: phase pylon, server spine, security plinth, light barrier, relay prism and damaged node.
- 3 decals: corrupted circuit, scan ring and warning glyph.
- 2 landmarks: sentinel aperture and twin phase pylons.

### Tactical Contract

- `ch1_m5` locked size: 16 x 12; steal data from the inner-ring terminal after the skills/items tutorial.
- `ch1_m6` locked size: 18 x 14; assassinate the Data Sentinel in a concentric arena with at least three breach lanes.
- The boss starts north of the aperture, while player deployment remains south of the outer ring.
- Core landmarks never hide target cells or fake collision; every blocker remains represented in the locked-map blocker layer.

## Import and Release

- Runtime PNGs live under `assets/generated/chapter1/runtime/environment/<kit>/`.
- Source style boards and unprocessed generation sheets live under `assets/generated/chapter1/source/` and are excluded by `export_presets.cfg`.
- Runtime files use PNG with exact pixel dimensions and alpha where appropriate.
- Every component path is registered through `ArtCatalog` and loaded by the release smoke test.
